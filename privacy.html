Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-07

Hermes is a private, single-user automation tool operated by its owner. It accesses only the owner's own Google account, including Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, and Google Contacts, in order to send email, manage calendar events, and manage files on the owner's behalf.

Data handling

  • Data accessed from Google is used solely to perform actions requested by the owner.
  • No data is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party.
  • Credentials and access tokens are stored locally on the owner's own device and are never transmitted to any third party.

Retention

The owner may revoke access at any time, which immediately ends collection and results in deletion of stored tokens.
