Hermes — Personal Automation App

Hermes is a private, single-user automation tool operated by its owner. It connects to the owner's own Google account (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Contacts) to send email, manage calendar events, and manage files on the owner's behalf.

It is not a commercial service and is not distributed to the public.
